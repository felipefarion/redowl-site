Primeira Terça · Tax Deed Support

Live · national

A data product that lets Brazilian investors research US tax-deed auctions - every fragmented public record, one decision-ready page.

  • 3,143 US counties mapped for auction availability, with state-law rules catalogued for all 51 jurisdictions
  • 1,271 property pages live across two states (Texas + Georgia) for the same auction day - list to full page in under a week per new state
  • 6-step due diligence on a single page: opening bid, official appraisal, map/Street View, flood zone, market estimate, deed - every data point with source and date
  • Data is audited, not just filled: sampled pages re-verified against official sources through independent channels; auction bids matched to the cent, zero divergences
  • Verification history shipped as UI: hover any data point to see when it was checked, how many times, and by which source
  • Registered deeds read by multimodal AI (OCR) to surface the real sale price Texas legally hides (non-disclosure state)
  • Re-collection caught a county rescheduling its auction before any user saw the wrong date
  • When federal flood-map servers went down mid-build (government shutdown), the pipeline re-routed to independent official mirrors - same data, cross-checked
  • Deterministic AI assistant: answers only from the database, cites records, says "not covered" instead of guessing
  • Newspaper-inspired dual interface - "study edition" for first-timers, "auction edition" for pros - in PT/EN/ES, with a glossary that explains US auction terms through Brazilian analogies

House rules - how the fleet is governed

Agents do the work. They never get the judgment.

Anyone can prompt an AI to write code. The hard part is trusting what comes back. Red Owl's answer is a written rulebook the fleet actually runs on:

RULE 01 - HUMAN HARD LINES

Money, access, personal data

No agent moves money, touches credentials or deletes personal data. Ever. Those three calls are made by a person, on the record.

RULE 02 - ADVERSARIAL GATES

Refute before merge

Before work ships, an independent agent tries to tear it apart - up to three passes. Nothing merges without a green verdict it failed to refute.

RULE 03 - BLIND CONTROLS

Honeypots in every audit

Audits include planted cases the auditor can't recognize. This is what prevents agent hallucinations - and why the numbers can be trusted.

RULE 04 - DECISIONS ON RECORD

ADRs, not vibes

Every architectural decision is written down and versioned. Agents consult the record before acting; nothing gets silently re-decided.
