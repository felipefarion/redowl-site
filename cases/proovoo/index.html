The challenge

Prove people actually wanted this, before building it.

Identity verification (KYC, "know your customer") to cut fraud in person-to-person deals was a hypothesis, not a certainty. The challenge was to validate real demand before committing to a full build, on a lean budget, with two different target audiences that might respond very differently.

The approach

Spend on real signal before spending on a product.

A fake-door test, a real landing page and a real sign-up flow, went live in production with real paid traffic from Google Ads, for two distinct anchor audiences, including the policy certification process the ad platform required for this niche. Product analytics (PostHog) tracked arrival, intent and conversion per audience, and an automated lifecycle (welcome, day 2, day 5, win-back) nudged signed-up users along without anyone sending a message by hand. Only once that signal was real did the studio's agent fleet build the iOS and Android apps, with OTP and a KYC provider integrated.

The terms, in plain language

FAKE-DOOR TEST

A real page, before the real build

A landing page and sign-up flow that looks and works like the real thing, live before the rest of the product exists, so real visitors from paid ads can show genuine interest before anything else gets built.

OTP

One-time password

A short code sent by text or app that confirms a person actually controls the phone number or account they claim.

KYC PROVIDER

Identity check, outsourced

A third-party service that checks a government ID against a live face scan, to confirm someone is who they say they are.

LIFECYCLE ENGINE

Messages that send themselves

Automatic messages timed after signup, welcome, day 2, day 5, win-back, that nudge a new user along without anyone sending them by hand.

Four ways to check before you trust

Scam check

Paste a suspicious message, print, audio or document; get an instant read on whether it looks like a scam.

Verified identity page

A page a service professional can show a client, proving a document and a live face check already happened.

Counterpart verification

Ask the other side of a deal to verify themselves, with consent, before money or a meeting is agreed.

Audio / video analysis

Check whether a suspicious voice or video message might have been generated by a computer.

How it was verified

Why the demand behind this can be trusted.

REAL MONEY, REAL SIGNAL

Interest was tested with real paid traffic, not a survey or a guess. Two separate audiences ran side by side, so the studio was comparing real behavior, not the loudest opinion in the room.

PLAYING BY THE PLATFORM'S RULES

The ad niche required an approved policy certification before campaigns could run at all, so the validation happened inside real constraints, not a workaround.

MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED

Arrival, intent and conversion were tracked per audience with product analytics, so the decision to build the full apps came from behavior, not opinion.

THE SAME HOUSE RULES

Money, access and personal data stayed human decisions throughout; an independent agent checked every piece of work before it shipped, the same governance that runs every product in this studio.
